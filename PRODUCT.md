# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

The owner approved Next.js App Router, TypeScript, native CSS, locally maintained content and limited client-side enhancement. The approved Resend contact feature replaces static-only export with a Next.js server and a native POST Route Handler; portfolio/project pages remain pre-rendered. Deployment provider and domain are open decisions; the current review is local.

## Users

Prospective clients are the primary audience. They need to understand James Gatoru’s software and agentic AI capabilities, inspect relevant real work and contact him. Employers are an occasional secondary audience evaluating his projects, skills and experience.

## Product Purpose

Present James Gatoru’s work, skills and professional experience in a distinctive, usable portfolio. Visitors can explore selected projects, browse the complete work archive, read individual project overviews, download a public-facing CV and contact James directly.

## Operating Context

The initial content comes from the two supplied CVs and the public James-Toru GitHub account. Content is maintained locally and published through a rebuild; browsing does not depend on live GitHub API requests. Visitors may arrive on phones, desktop computers or lower-tier devices, with or without JavaScript or decorative motion.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The current collection contains 13 curated records assembled from CV work, public repositories and the owner's 2026-11-03 additions and follow-up removals. LawPro360, DOLMIS, CargoForward360 and Nashami Limited are featured. IGC Portal and Kalamu have supplied public website links and real previews. The owner removed the specified projects, repositories, forks and supporting section; the original research inventory remains historical evidence rather than a publishing requirement.
- Search by project or technology and category filters enhance the archive. All records and direct project links remain available in the initial HTML.
- Contact keeps the confirmed email, gatorumbugua@gmail.com, and verified profile links, plus a shared enquiry form with required name, email and message. A server-only Resend endpoint fixes the recipient and sender; the visitor's validated email is Reply-To. Pending requests freeze fields and prevent duplicate submits; failed drafts remain available for retry. Success means provider acceptance for delivery. Missing configuration reports unavailable, and the direct email link works without scripting. There is no visitor account system, database, CMS, attachment or automatic reply.
- The owner approved original layered city artwork and lightweight, optional effects. A real-time 3D renderer, video background, forced intro, scroll interception and audio are outside the first release.
- Keep the original CVs unchanged. At the owner's explicit request, the download serves an exact copy of the supplied James Gatoru Curriculum Vitae PDF, including its existing contents. Site contact configuration remains unchanged.
- No deployment, hosting account or paid service is required for local form review. Live delivery needs owner-configured server secrets and a verified Resend sender. A production host must run Next.js server code and supply origin configuration and host-level abuse controls; static-file-only hosting cannot run the contact endpoint. Hosting and domain selection remain undecided.

## Brand Commitments

The owner selected Exo 2 for body text on 2026-11-03 while retaining Barlow Condensed for display headings. Serve both families locally.

James Gatoru is the portfolio identity; James / Toru is the implemented wordmark. The owner selected the Midnight Network direction and approved its desktop and mobile concepts. Preserve its immersive blue, cyan and cobalt city identity and the explicit rejection of acid yellow. ## Product Principles

- Make relevant work easy to inspect and direct contact easy to reach.
- Represent the owner's curated collection honestly, preserving the difference between professional work, independent projects and experiments.
- Keep core content available before client enhancement and without decorative motion.
- Preserve the portfolio’s identity across screen sizes while prioritizing readable content and usable controls.
- Add new projects through evidence-backed local content rather than runtime service dependencies.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Full responsiveness and usability on mobile and lower-tier devices are explicit owner requirements. Support keyboard and touch navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, meaningful image alternatives, natural reflow and reduced-motion preferences. Effects Auto respects operating-system reduced motion; Off stops nonessential decoration and persists locally when storage is available. Information must not depend on hover or animation. Verification evidence and outstanding test limitations belong in verification records rather than being treated as proof of universal device performance.
