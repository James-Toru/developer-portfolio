# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

The owner approved Next.js App Router, TypeScript and a static export, with native CSS, locally maintained content and limited client-side enhancement. Project pages are generated at build time. Deployment provider and domain are open decisions; the current review is local.

## Users

Prospective clients are the primary audience. They need to understand James Gatoru’s software and agentic AI capabilities, inspect relevant real work and contact him. Employers are an occasional secondary audience evaluating his projects, skills and experience.

## Product Purpose

Present James Gatoru’s work, skills and professional experience in a distinctive, usable portfolio. Visitors can explore selected projects, browse the complete work archive, read individual project overviews, download a public-facing CV and contact James directly.

## Operating Context

The initial content comes from the two supplied CVs and the public James-Toru GitHub account. Content is maintained locally and published through a rebuild; browsing does not depend on live GitHub API requests. Visitors may arrive on phones, desktop computers or lower-tier devices, with or without JavaScript or decorative motion.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The current collection contains 28 public-facing records assembled from CV work and public repositories. The archive distinguishes professional work, independent projects, experiments, forks and supporting repositories; a complete inventory is not a claim of 28 shipped client products.
- Search by project or technology and category filters enhance the archive. All records and direct project links remain available in the initial HTML.
- Contact uses the confirmed email, gatorumbugua@gmail.com, and verified profile links. There is no message-sending backend, account system, database or CMS.
- The owner approved original layered city artwork and lightweight, optional effects. A real-time 3D renderer, video background, forced intro, scroll interception and audio are outside the first release.
- Keep the original CVs unchanged. The downloadable public CV is a derivative with the confirmed email and without referee contacts; no public telephone is required.
- No deployment, hosting account or paid service is required for local review. Hosting and domain selection remain undecided.

## Brand Commitments

James Gatoru is the portfolio identity; James / Toru is the implemented wordmark. The owner selected the Midnight Network direction and approved its desktop and mobile concepts. Preserve its immersive blue, cyan and cobalt city identity and the explicit rejection of acid yellow. The RAXOS repository is an implementation reference, while the selected Midnight Network concepts remain the visual authority. Detailed visual rules belong in DESIGN.md.

## Product Principles

- Make relevant work easy to inspect and direct contact easy to reach.
- Represent the complete source collection honestly, preserving the difference between original work, experiments, forks and supporting resources.
- Keep core content available before client enhancement and without decorative motion.
- Preserve the portfolio’s identity across screen sizes while prioritizing readable content and usable controls.
- Add new projects through evidence-backed local content rather than runtime service dependencies.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Full responsiveness and usability on mobile and lower-tier devices are explicit owner requirements. Support keyboard and touch navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, meaningful image alternatives, natural reflow and reduced-motion preferences. Effects Auto respects operating-system reduced motion; Off stops nonessential decoration and persists locally when storage is available. Information must not depend on hover or animation. Verification evidence and outstanding test limitations belong in verification records rather than being treated as proof of universal device performance.
