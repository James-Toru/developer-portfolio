Composition: asymmetric editorial typographic layout with acid yellow as an architectural dominant field at the left, near-black lower work area, large original rain-lit futuristic city visual at upper right with a restrained red architectural scanning overlay integrated into it. City imagery should be dramatic and detailed, not vague neon gradient. Wide functional top navigation. Name is enormous and condensed, in two strong lines, with deliberate cuts in the typography. Red structural rules, a few tiny cyan technical annotations and crisp precise metadata. Plenty of breathing room around crucial copy. Industrial textures only in illustration, keep text sharp. The city and graphic structure should feel native to this portfolio, not a rectangle placed inside a dashboard. No rounded cards.

Thumbnails should suggest a node flow diagram, a document/workflow portal and a web page respectively, without fabricated metrics or customer claims. CONCEPT 2 — THE SUMMER HIKARU DIED INSPIRED

Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: one high-fidelity desktop homepage visual concept for a developer portfolio.
Primary request: Create the separate THE SUMMER HIKARU DIED inspired design for James Gatoru, software developer and AI agentic engineer in Kenya. Capture the specific tension of luminous rural summer, blue skies, leafy green shadows, expressive manga ink, nostalgic quiet and a faint uncanny undertow. Entirely independent of cyberpunk; this direction is a mature illustrated anime and literary editorial website, art-directed and client-ready. Original scenery, no existing characters, show title, logos or promotional poster elements.
Format: landscape 1536 x 1024. Flat straight-on real webpage design edge to edge, no browser chrome, laptop frame, moodboard collage or explanatory swatches.
Visual world: warm ivory paper, midnight ink typography, luminous cerulean sky, saturated dense forest green, a tiny hand-drawn coral-red accent. Fine manga hatching and organic paper grain, elegant high-contrast editorial serif for the name, clean readable modern sans for essential copy. Spacious layout with exceptionally composed margins and delicate little handwritten annotations. Not cute chibi, not pastel template, not fantasy video-game UI.
Composition: a thin warm-paper masthead. A cinematic upper section around 560px high where the left third remains quiet ivory for strong text and the original anime scenery envelops the right two thirds, with an irregular hand-painted ink boundary. Scene: an empty sunlit path beside a still irrigation canal, enormous summer clouds, tangled rural foliage and power lines, painted with film-quality anime background craft and precise inkwork; the calm water holds a subtly impossible deep-ink reflection, suggesting mystery without gore or monsters. A leafy shadow spills over the paper edge and the illustration blends into the page. Scene must have compelling composition, lush color and specific crafted details.
